\begin{abstract}
    In this paper we show the first constant-time and fully masked implementation of HQC targeting Cortex-M$4$ microcontrollers. 
    Our proposal for a masked implementation is constant-time and
    has a less-than linear overhead factor in the number of shares, thanks to 
    appropriate exploitations of the Boolean linearity of the components
    of HQC.
    We demostrate the efficiency of our implementation and its constant-time 
    behavior concretely, through taking clock-cycle level timing and 
    electromagnetic emission measures on a commercial grade Cortex-M$4$, the 
    current NIST reference microcontroller platform.
\keywords{Post-quantum cryptography, code-based cryptosystems, constant time
implementation, masking}
\end{abstract}
