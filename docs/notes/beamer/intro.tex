\begin{frame}{Cryptanalytic attacks}
    What are the best attacks on modern cryptosystems?
    \begin{block}{On Classical Computers}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Symmetric: exhaustive search, $\mathcal{O}(2^n)$
            \item Asymmetric: General Number Field Sieve, $\approx \mathcal{O}(2^{n-\epsilon})$
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}

    \begin{block}{On Quantum Computers}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Symmetric: Grover's algorithm, $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{2^n})$
            \item Asymmetric: Shor's algorithm, $\approx \mathcal{O}((\log{n})^2)$
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
    Shor's algorithm can break asymmetric cryptosystems in polynomial time!
\end{frame}
